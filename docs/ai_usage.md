## What AI Was Used For

- Some testing examples, especially benchmark-style examples such as `buggy_snake`, were created with AI assistance and then reviewed and adjusted by us.
- Some prompts used in the harness prompt files were drafted with AI assistance and then edited for the final system.
- The testing-analysis workflow was developed with AI as a source of design ideas and references, but the final workflow was not copied directly from AI output and was selectively adopted and revised by us.
- Some analysis and documentation work also used AI as a reference tool, but the final interpretation and project decisions were not produced entirely by AI.

## What AI Was Not Used For

- The project was not built by simply copying full AI output end to end.
- Final system design choices, evaluation framing, and project conclusions were reviewed and decided by the team.
- AI was used as assistance and reference.
